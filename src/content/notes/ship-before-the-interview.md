I was applying regularly on Naukri, Internshala and company websites, and giving interviews. It was mostly rejections: some take-home assignments, a few replies.

Then one application clicked. **SystemStar** emailed me interview slots along with *detailed expectations for the role*. That email told me more than the job post had.

Instead of just confirming a slot, **I built a small mock aligned with what they actually wanted, and shared it.**

They reviewed it and found issues, some of them device-specific. I fixed them and sent updates. We went back and forth like that over several emails.

By the time the interview happened, they already knew how I worked. The call was mostly about where to take the product next.

## What I took from it

- **Don't wait to feel ready** before applying.
- **Job descriptions are incomplete by default.**
- **Showing work early changes the outcome.**

I'm still a student and still learning. But how you engage *before* the interview can matter more than the interview itself.
